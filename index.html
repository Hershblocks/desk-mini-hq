Three steps to a call

  1. Plug the Desk Mini into your laptop with the USB-C cable.
  2. Open your call app and go to its audio and video settings.
  3. Pick Desk Mini for the camera, the microphone and the speaker.

Your laptop makes the call. The Desk Mini is the camera, mic and speaker.

If something is off

Quick fixes

No picture

In the call app's video settings, switch the camera to Desk Mini. If it isn't listed, unplug the cable, wait five seconds and plug it back in.

People can't hear me

Switch the microphone to Desk Mini and check that the app isn't muted.

I can't hear them

Switch the speaker to Desk Mini and turn the laptop volume up.

There's an echo

Use the Desk Mini for both mic and speaker. Mixing it with the laptop's own mic or speakers causes echo.

Optional

Calling without a laptop

The Desk Mini needs to be signed in to Webex once before it can place calls by itself.

  1. Sign in to your own Webex account and open Webex Settings.
  2. Choose My Devices, then Generate Activation Code.
  3. On the Desk Mini, choose Cisco Webex and type the code.

Not confirmed: whether a free Webex account can make the code, and whether signing in changes the name or wallpaper.
